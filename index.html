How I work

From audit to stable operation

  1. 01

    Audit

    Site visit, water analysis and operating data. I find where the process loses performance.

  2. 02

    Analysis

    Mass balances, process modelling in GPS-X, design checks and calculations against standards and supplier data.

  3. 03

    Solution

    A specific engineering fix or a new design: process, sizing, equipment and budget.

  4. 04

    Support

    Commissioning, operator training and follow-up until the results hold.

What I work with

Water and wastewater treatment, stage by stage

Water treatment
01

Filtration

Removing suspended solids and turbidity and preparing water for softening and membranes.

02

Softening

Reducing hardness for process water, boilers and heat exchange equipment.

03

Membranes

Ultrafiltration and reverse osmosis: selection, calculation, commissioning and cleaning regimes.

04

Chemical treatment

Choosing reagents and dosing points for the actual water composition.

05

Disinfection

Hypochlorite and gas chlorine: dose calculation, dosing units and residual chlorine control.

Wastewater treatment
01

Mechanical

Screens, grit removal and equalisation protect the next stages from debris and shock loads.

02

Physico-chemical (DAF)

Flotation with chemical treatment removes fats, suspended solids and part of the COD.

03

Biological

Aerobic treatment, including SBR and MBR: aeration, cycles and sludge age.

04

Sludge dewatering

Dewatering, including decanter centrifuges: installation, commissioning and service.
