Formula
Dose = demand + residual
Cl₂ (g/h) = dose (mg/L) × Q (m³/h)
Strength (g/L) = trade % × 10
Solution (L/h) = Cl₂ (g/h) / strength (g/L)
Sources
  • AWWA Ohio Section, White Paper on Disinfection with Hypochlorites (2007)
    Dosage equation (section 2.5.2) and definition of trade percent: 200 g/L available chlorine is 20 trade percent. Metric form of the same equations. The paper cites AWWA B300-04; the current edition of the hypochlorite standard is ANSI/AWWA B300-24.
Limits and assumptions
  • Find the chlorine demand by a chlorine demand test on your water. It changes with temperature, organics and contact time (AWWA Ohio 2007, section 2.2).
  • Sodium hypochlorite loses available chlorine in storage, faster with heat and light. Use the measured strength, not the delivery note (AWWA Ohio 2007, section 2.4).
  • Trade percent means grams of available chlorine per 100 mL. If your supplier gives percent by weight, convert with the solution density.
  • Required residual and contact time are set by your local regulations. They are not built into this calculator.
