Gas chlorination for the upgrade of two drinking water plants

I am designing a gas chlorination system for the upgrade of plants built in the 1970s–80s. Each plant treats over 15,000 m³/h.

Industry
Drinking water
Technologies
Gas chlorine, Disinfection
Status
In progress, launch 2027
01

Input data

Capacity of each plantover 15,000 m³/h
Reagentliquid chlorine in 1 t containers
My roleprocess engineer: calculations, equipment selection, P&ID, specification, safety section, scrubbers for both plants, control logic; then installation supervision and commissioning
02

Engineering challenge

The plants were built in the 1970s–80s, and the chlorine system has to be replaced. The new system must hold the set dose accurately, leave headroom for capacity growth and stay safe along the whole chlorine path, from storage to injection into the water.

03

Solution

A vacuum gas chlorination system with standby at every stage, from the container store to the ejectors at the injection points. The diagram below shows how it works.

04

Result

The solution step by step: chlorine path from storage to water

  1. 01

    Storage

    1 t containers

    Two container racks on scales with automatic changeover. Stock sized for operation at maximum dose.

  2. 02

    Liquid chlorine feed

    liquid phase

    Seamless steel piping, motorised valves and pressure monitoring. Overpressure protection on the lines.

  3. 03

    Vaporisation and pressure reduction

    duty + standby

    Electric vaporisers, gas filter, pressure reducing and relief valves.

  4. 04

    Vacuum dosing

    under vacuum

    Vacuum regulators with standby and shut-off on vacuum loss. Chlorinators dose in proportion to water flow.

  5. 05

    Ejection and injection

    on motive water

    Ejectors, a flowmeter and check valves on every line. Injection for pre-oxidation and disinfection.

Safety

Chlorine leak detectors, audible and visual alarms. Automatic emergency shut-off of the containers on a signal from a detector, button, SCADA or fire alarm. Scrubbers neutralise chlorine releases.

Control

A local PLC with an operator panel linked to the plant PLC. The dose is paced by water flow; residual chlorine is measured continuously.

My role

Process calculations, equipment selection, P&ID, specification, safety section, scrubber design for both plants. Supervision of the 3D model and layout, control logic for the programmers and its review.
