Scheduled maintenance of a GEA decanter in one working day

Carried out scheduled maintenance of a GEA waterMaster CF 4000 decanter at a food plant wastewater facility. We stripped the bowl and scroll, replaced worn parts and reassembled the machine in one working day. The vibration the centrifuge had been running with was gone after the service.

Engineering challenge

The centrifuge was running with vibration. The task was to carry out scheduled maintenance and return the decanter to operation without vibration.

03

Solution

Scheduled maintenance with full disassembly: bearings, seals, drive shaft and belts replaced from a 27-item parts list, wear checked, units reassembled to the manufacturer drawing. The sequence is shown below.
