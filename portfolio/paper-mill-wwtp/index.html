Wastewater treatment plant for a paper mill, 2,160 m³/day

Installation supervision and commissioning of a wastewater treatment plant for a tissue paper mill: screening, chemical DAF, biological treatment and sludge dewatering. Treated water meets the limits for discharge to the municipal sewer.

Industry
Pulp and paper
Technologies
Screening, DAF, Biological treatment, Sludge dewatering
Year
2024
01

Input data

Capacity2,160 m³/day
Peak discharge from the paper machine240 m³/h for 6 hours
Treated water requirementmunicipal sewer discharge limits
My roleService engineer
02

Engineering challenge

Effluent arrives unevenly: the paper machine discharges up to 240 m³/h for 6 hours. The water has to meet the utility limits for discharge to the municipal sewer, and no untreated water may leave the plant.

03

Solution

Full-cycle treatment: screening, chemical DAF and biology, with automatic monitoring at every stage. The diagram below shows how it works.

04

Result

Commissioning completedNovember 2024

The solution step by step: effluent path from the mill to the sewer

  1. 01

    Screening

    drum screens

    Remove fibre and coarse solids.

  2. 02

    Equalisation

    peak up to 240 m³/h

    Mixed and aerated tanks absorb the paper machine peak discharge.

  3. 03

    Chemical DAF

    KWI Megacell DAF

    Coagulant and flocculant, pH correction, solids removal.

  4. 04

    Biological treatment

    2 bioreactors

    Turbo aerators. Ammonia water and phosphoric acid as nitrogen and phosphorus sources.

  5. 05

    Sludge separation and discharge

    second Megacell DAF

    Separates activated sludge. Treated water goes to the municipal sewer.

Sludge

Float sludge and excess sludge are dewatered on Polat decanter centrifuges; the centrate returns to equalisation.

Monitoring

COD, pH and dissolved oxygen sensors. Off-spec water is automatically sent back to the emergency equalisation tank.

My role

Service engineer. Installation supervision, DAF tuning, chemical dose selection, taking part in the biological start-up, sampling for analysis, staff training.
