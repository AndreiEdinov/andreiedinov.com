Flocculant solution preparation units, 3×3,000 L/h, for a pulp and paper mill

Installation supervision and commissioning of three automatic flocculant preparation units at a pulp and paper mill. The solution is used to retain fine fibre on the paper machine and to dewater sludge on filter presses.

Industry
Pulp and paper
Technologies
Flocculation, Fibre retention, Sludge dewatering

02

Engineering challenge

On the paper machine, part of the fine fibre is lost with the white water, and the mill loses raw material. The sludge, fibre reject and biological sludge, is dewatered on filter presses, and the load on them had to come down.

03

Solution

A line of three automatic units prepares solution from powder flocculant continuously: wetting, maturing with a slow-speed mixer and gentle feed by progressive cavity pumps. The diagram below shows how it works.

04

Result

The solution step by step: from powder to the users

  1. 01

    Powder dosing

    dry flocculant

    Powder flocculant is fed into the unit in metered amounts.

  2. 02

    Wetting and maturing

    3 × 3,000 L/h

    The powder is wetted in a tangential mixer, then the solution matures in a tank with a slow-speed mixer.

  3. 03

    Dosing

    progressive cavity pumps

    Progressive cavity pumps feed the ready solution to the users gently, without breaking the polymer molecules.

  4. 04

    Retention on the paper machine

    fine fibre

    The flocculant binds fine fibre into flocs, so it stays in the paper web instead of leaving with the white water.

  5. 05

    Sludge dewatering

    filter presses

    The flocculant makes the sludge particles larger, and they give up water more easily on the filter presses.

Lump-free wetting

In the tangential mixer water enters along the wall, and the powder is wetted evenly, without lumps.

Tank maintenance

The unit tanks are made of HDPE. Sealed hatches give access for inspection and maintenance.

My role

Service engineer. Installation supervision, commissioning, tuning of the unit control system and staff training.
