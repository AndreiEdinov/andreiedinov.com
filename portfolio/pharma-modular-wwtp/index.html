I designed a 50 m³/day wastewater treatment module for a pharmaceutical plant. The module was assembled and tested at the factory before shipment, so on site it took 3 working days to install and 2 to commission.

Engineering challenge

A pharmaceutical plant needed a 50 m³/day treatment plant fast: no lengthy construction on site and start-up within days. Treated water is discharged to the ground, so treatment had to reach steady performance right after start-up.

03

Solution

All treatment except equalisation sits in one module. It was assembled and tested at the factory before shipment, so installation and start-up took 5 working days. The diagram below shows how it works.

The solution step by step: effluent path from the plant to discharge

  1. 01

    Equalisation

    50 m³

    Effluent flows by gravity into a tank with a Flygt jet aerator.

  2. 02

    Chemicals

    coagulant + flocculant

    Pumps feed the module; mixing in the coagulation chamber.

  3. 03

    DAF

    KWI DAF Minicell

    Removes suspended solids and flocs.

  4. 04

    Polishing and discharge

    multimedia filters

    Clarified water is discharged to the ground.

Sludge

DAF sludge is dewatered in filter bags.
