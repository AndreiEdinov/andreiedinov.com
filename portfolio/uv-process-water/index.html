Modular UV sterilisation unit for process water, 2,500 L/h

I am designing a modular UV sterilisation unit for process water, 2,500 L/h, for a food plant. The unit is assembled and tested at the factory, so on site installation takes 1 day and commissioning 1 day.

Industry
Food processing
Technologies
UV disinfection, Filtration, Automation

My roleLead project engineer
02

Engineering challenge

Borehole water is used to wash floors in the production area. Untreated water must not reach production if the lamp fails, if power is lost, or after pauses in demand. Work on site has to take as little time as possible.

03

Solution

A modular unit: pumps, filters, UV reactor, valves and control panel on one skid. It is tested at the factory and arrives ready; on site only the tank, water and power need connecting. The scheme below shows how it works.

Step by step: from the tank to production

  1. 01

    Storage

    2 m³

    Tank topped up from the borehole by level switches. The low switch protects the pumps from running dry.

  2. 02

    Pump group

    duty + standby

    28 m head, pumps alternate every 24 h. Flow is set on a rotameter.

  3. 03

    Pre-filtration

    90 µm

    A backwashable screen filter protects the lamp quartz sleeve.

  4. 04

    UV disinfection

    90 W · UV sensor

    Intensity sensor: below 50 % the water goes to drain and the line to production closes.

  5. 05

    Guard filter and supply

    30 µm

    Catches sleeve fragments after a failure. Water goes to production or back to the tank.

Safety

All solenoid valves are normally closed: without power the line to production is shut. On a UV alarm the unit flushes to drain and stops the pump. The UV bypass is padlocked. Siemens LOGO! controller; the emergency stop cuts pumps and valves in hardware.

Speed of start-up

The module is assembled, piped and tested at the factory. On site the plan is 1 working day for installation and 1 for commissioning.

My role

Lead project engineer. Process scheme and P&ID, equipment selection, 3D module layout, detailed design and automation specifications, manual in Russian and English. Installation supervision and commissioning.
