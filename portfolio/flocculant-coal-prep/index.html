Flocculant preparation units, 3,000 L/h, for a coal preparation plant

Installation supervision and commissioning of automatic flocculant preparation units at a coal preparation plant. The solution is used to clarify recycled process water in thickeners and radial clarifiers. The job took 5 working days.

Industry
Coal processing

02

Engineering challenge

Coal washing leaves the water loaded with fine solids: coal slimes and clay. Without chemicals these particles take days to settle, and the plant needs clarified water to send back into the process.

03

Solution

Automatic units prepare solution from powder flocculant for the thickeners and radial clarifiers. Two tanks work in turn, so the solution is supplied without interruption. The diagram below shows how it works.

04

Result

The solution step by step: the recycled water path

  1. 01

    Coal washing

    water with slimes

    Water leaves the process carrying coal slimes and clay.

  2. 02

    Solution preparation

    up to 3,000 L/h

    An automatic unit on top of the tank feeds dry flocculant and mixes it with water. There are two tanks: one prepares the solution while the other doses the ready one, then they swap.

  3. 03

    Flocculation

    minutes, not days

    The flocculant binds fine particles into large flocs that settle within minutes.

  4. 04

    Thickening

    thickeners, radial clarifiers

    Flocs settle to the bottom; thickened slurry is pumped out from below.

  5. 05

    Water return

    closed loop

    Clarified water overflows at the top and returns to the start of the process.

My role

Service engineer. Installation supervision, commissioning and tuning of the flocculant preparation units, staff training.
